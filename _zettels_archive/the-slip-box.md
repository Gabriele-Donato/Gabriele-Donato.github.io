---
title: "Why a Slip-Box and Not a Notebook"
note_id: "1"
date: 2026-01-11
tags: [method, writing]
excerpt: "A notebook is ordered by when you had the thought. A slip-box is ordered by what the thought is next to, which is the only order that can surprise you."
---

A notebook is ordered by *when* you had the thought. That order is useless: it
is a fact about your calendar, not about the idea.

A slip-box is ordered by what a note sits next to. Since nothing can be filed
without deciding what it belongs beside, the filing itself is the thinking, and
the box can hand back a juxtaposition you did not put there on purpose.

The rule that does the work is the boring one: one idea per note, written so a
stranger could read it, and never filed without at least one link.
