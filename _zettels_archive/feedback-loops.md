A loop is either balancing — it pushes the system back towards a set point —
or reinforcing, where the output feeds the input and the thing runs away from
you. Almost every argument about incentives is really a disagreement about
which of the two is in charge.

Two practical corollaries:

- Delay turns a balancing loop into an oscillation. The thermostat that reads
  the temperature an hour late does not stabilise the room, it makes it swing.
- Reinforcing loops are indifferent to the sign of what they amplify. The same
  structure produces compound interest and a bank run.

Habits are the reinforcing case with a very short delay, which is precisely
why they are so hard to argue with once installed.
